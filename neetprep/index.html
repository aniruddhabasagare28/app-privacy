NEET Prep AI – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

NEET Prep AI ("the app") is an Android app developed by Aniruddha, an individual developer. It helps students prepare for the NEET (UG) exam with NCERT chapters, AI-written study guides and practice tests, an AI tutor, progress tracking, exam reminders and study breaks. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Your name, class, exam date, progress and settings stay on your phone.
  • When you use an AI feature, the text of your request is sent to the AI service you chose, using your own API key.
  • The developer has no server and never receives your data, except a report you choose to send.

Information stored on your phone

  • Profile: the first name and class you enter, your NEET exam date and start time, and your reminder and break settings.
  • Study data: test results, bookmarks, studied chapters, streaks and saved AI study guides.
  • Your AI key: the API key you paste in is encrypted with the Android Keystore and stored only on this phone. It is excluded from backups and is never sent to the developer.
  • NCERT books: chapter PDFs you download from ncert.nic.in are kept on the phone for offline reading.

If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may back up your profile and study data to your Google account. Your AI key and downloaded books are excluded. Backup is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by the app.

Information sent to AI services

Tests, study guides, explanations and tutor answers are written by an AI service that you choose in the app: Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude), OpenRouter, or another OpenAI-compatible service you enter. When you use these features, the app sends that service:

  • your request (for example a chapter name, the topics for a test, a passage you asked to have explained, or your tutor question and the conversation so far), and
  • your API key, so the service knows the request is yours.

Requests go directly from your phone to that service over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The developer does not see them. The service handles them under its own privacy policy and terms, which you accepted when you created your key: Google, Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter. Please don't type personal information such as your phone number or address into the AI tutor.

Other connections

  • NCERT: chapter books are downloaded from ncert.nic.in, and the app checks it for revised chapters.
  • Syllabus updates: the app may download an updated syllabus list from this website (GitHub Pages). No information about you is sent.

These are ordinary web requests. Like any website, those servers can see your phone's IP address.

Reports

Every AI answer has a Report button. If you send a report, it contains the reason you picked, any details you type, where in the app it happened, the app version, and the AI text you are reporting. It goes to the developer by email or an online form and is used only to fix problems and improve safety. Reports do not include your name, your key or your progress.

Notifications

With your permission, the app shows exam countdown reminders and study-break alarms. They are created on your phone; no information is sent anywhere to schedule them.

Deleting your data

NEET Prep AI has no accounts and no server, so almost everything is on your phone and you can delete it yourself at any time:

  • Test results, bookmarks, streaks and studied chapters: open Settings → Reset all progress. They are deleted at once.
  • Downloaded NCERT books: Settings → Storage → Delete books. Saved AI study guides: Settings → Storage → Clear AI guides.
  • Everything at once, including your name, class, exam date and AI key: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's app settings.
  • Reports you sent with the Report button: email aniruddhabasagare28@gmail.com with the subject "NEET Prep AI data deletion" and roughly when you sent the report. It is deleted within 30 days. Reports you don't ask to delete are kept only as long as needed to fix the problem, and at most 12 months.
  • Requests you sent to an AI service (Gemini, Groq, ChatGPT, Claude, OpenRouter): the developer never receives these. The AI service keeps them under its own policy; delete them, or your key, on that service's website.

Children

The app is meant for students aged 13 and over preparing for NEET. It is not directed at children under 13. Students under 18 should have a parent or guardian's permission before creating an AI key, as the AI services' own terms may require.

Not an official app

NEET Prep AI is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting on behalf of the National Testing Agency (NTA), NCERT or the Government of India. Check exam dates and rules on neet.nta.nic.in. AI-written content can contain mistakes, so always check facts with your NCERT textbook.
