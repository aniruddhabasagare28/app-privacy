In-Hand Salary – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

In-Hand Salary ("the app") is an Android app developed by Aniruddha, an individual developer. It works out an estimate of monthly take-home salary from a CTC under India's new and old income-tax regimes. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or sensitive data.

  • The salary figures and deductions you type in are used only on your device to do the calculation. They are not sent anywhere.
  • The app does not request the Internet permission or any other Android permission, so it cannot send data off your device.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Data stored on your device

While the app is open, Android may keep your last inputs so they are not lost when you rotate the screen or switch apps. Android's own device backup may include app data if you have backup turned on in your phone settings; that backup is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by this app. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the device.

Children

The app is meant for working adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Not tax advice and not a government app

Results are estimates for resident individuals below 60 years of age. Actual tax deducted depends on your employer's payroll and your declarations. The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or acting on behalf of the Income Tax Department or the Government of India. Tax slabs and limits are taken from the Finance Act as published at incometax.gov.in.

Changes to this policy
