Questions

Is NEET Prep AI free?

Yes. The app is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. The AI features use your own AI key: Google Gemini and Groq offer free daily allowances, which are enough for regular study. Paid services such as ChatGPT or Claude also work if you already have them.

What is an AI key, and is it safe?

An AI key is like a password that lets the app use an AI service on your behalf. You create it once on the AI company's website and paste it into the app. It is stored encrypted on your phone and is sent only to that AI service. Students under 18 should ask a parent or guardian to help create it.

Which classes and subjects are covered?

Physics, Chemistry and Biology from the current NCERT books for Classes 11 and 12, plus Science for Classes 8, 9 and 10 to strengthen your basics.

Does it work offline?

Yes, partly. Downloaded NCERT chapters, saved study guides, your progress, exam reminders and study breaks all work offline. New tests, guides and tutor answers need an internet connection.

Does the app have previous years' NEET papers?

No. Official NEET papers belong to the NTA, so the app doesn't copy or imitate them. Its mock tests are clearly AI-written practice in the NEET format, with +4/−1 marking.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is on your phone, so you can delete it yourself in Settings or by uninstalling the app. See Deleting your data for the exact steps.
